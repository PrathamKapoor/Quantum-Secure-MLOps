Renewable energy integration introduces variability and forecasting challenges that complicate grid operations. Traditional MLOps pipelines lack the governance and adaptability required for safe, self-adaptive forecasting in safety-critical smart grid applications. This work presents a guardrailed agentic MLOps system designed for self-adaptive energy forecasting in renewable-integrated smart grids. The solution combines a deterministic governance engine with bounded agentic AI to manage the forecasting lifecycle, including data validation, feature engineering, model training, drift monitoring, governed retraining, and champion-challenger evaluation. The system ensures safety through cryptographic model passports, quantum-secure verification, and policy-based agent actions that prevent unsafe operations. Contributions include a formalized forecasting lifecycle management framework, drift-aware retraining policies, and a verifiable agentic AI layer that operates within strict governance bounds. The final evaluation demonstrates successful execution of inference-only evaluation on frozen models, validating the pipeline's ability to generate forecasts and compute standard metrics (MAE, RMSE, sMAPE, nMAE, nRMSE) without retraining, hyperparameter optimization, or modification of models, features, or governance policies.
